These are non-negotiable:

• Know your data structures: arrays, maps, stacks, queues.
• Learn key algorithms: sorting, searching, recursion, graphs.
• Read the error messages: 99% of the time, they tell you what’s wrong.
• Get good at debugging: logs and breakpoints are your friends.
• Understand database design: tables, indexes, relationships.
• Know how HTTP works: headers, methods, status codes.
• Understand sync vs async, especially when things get slow.
• Write tests that catch bugs, not just for coverage.
• Read the docs; most issues are config, not code.
